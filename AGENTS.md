# Paper editing

- Reuse the paper's existing terminology, symbols, indices, and notation consistently across prose and equations. Do not introduce new terminology, notation, index sets, aliases, or reindexings unless the user explicitly requests them. If existing notation cannot express a requested addition, explain the issue before introducing anything new.
- Keep edits limited to the requested scope; preserve surrounding text and established notation.
- Never undo the user's edits unless explicitly requested. Read the current file before editing and treat it as authoritative, including punctuation, formatting, and paragraph openings.
- Preserve the user's equation punctuation and continuation style: do not restore removed commas or periods at the ends of displayed equations, and do not change continuations such as "where..." to "Here..." or otherwise rewrite them unless requested.
- Keep mathematical explanations concise. Define requested additions using the existing notation rather than creating parallel formulations.
